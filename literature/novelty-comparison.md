# Calibration novelty comparison

**Assessment, 19 November 2026.** The reviewed sources already establish held-out noise calibration of CNN scores, edge quantization, and empirical USRP data collection. The narrower candidate question is how gain-dependent movements across discrete INT8 scores affect detection and false alarms across receiver days under a fixed H0 budget. The fresh synthetic benchmark found no advantage for the proposed linear threshold rule. Priority remains unresolved.

| Study and evidence location | Established overlap | Boundary relevant to this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Sensors 2024, pp. 11, 18 and 20](papers/sensors-24-07907.pdf) | Fig. 3 and Table 3 describe validation accuracy and the BatchNorm architecture. Table 4 lists the 14,881-parameter GAP model. Fig. 15 reports accuracy by SNR. | §7 describes simulation and identifies hardware comparison as future work. These results do not establish cross-day Pd at a fixed false-alarm limit. |
| [Abushahla et al., IEEE Communications Letters 2025, indexed abstract](https://doi.org/10.1109/LCOMM.2025.3582680) | QAT, quantization scaling and Sony Spresense deployment. The authors report a 72% model-size reduction and faster inference. | Full text unavailable through the checked routes. The detector decision threshold, H0 budget, handling of tied scores and gain transfer remain unresolved. Hardware deployment and quantization are established overlap. |
| [Deshpande et al., §2.2, §4, Table 4 and §4.4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13369-026-11704-1) | §2.2 sets detector-specific thresholds using a held-out H0 empirical quantile, including CNN sigmoid scores. Table 4 specifies 256 complex samples, 20,000 frames per SNR, ±2 dB noise uncertainty and a 10% target Pfa. | §4.4 describes synthetic impairment data and anticipates measured SDR captures. Table 4 gives total frame counts, which do not specify an equal per-gain H0 calibration budget. The passages examined do not establish cross-day, discrete INT8 calibration performance. |
| [Compressive cyclostationary spectrum sensing with a constant false alarm rate, §§5–6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13638-017-0920-5) | Classical cyclostationary detection and numerical CFAR evaluation. | Relevant prior art for false-alarm-controlled detection. The detector statistic differs from a quantized CNN output score. |
| [A Lightweight CNN With Dilations for Spectrum Sensing Using Experimentally Collected Datasets, indexed abstract](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2026.3658247) | Two USRP B210s, wired and real-world datasets, and reported differences between synthetic and real performance. | USRP collection and simulation-to-hardware comparison already have direct precedent. Threshold budget, score quantization and cross-day evaluation were unresolved at abstract level. |
| [Wavelet-driven Partial Binary Quantization for accelerating deep-learning-based spectrum sensing, bibliographic record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sysarc.2026.103842) | Embedded spectrum sensing and partial binary quantization are explicit in the title. | Methods and calibration protocol require full-text review. This record alone cannot establish whether its detector threshold transfers between gains. |
